**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit **Bitdoze** ([https://www.bitdoze.com](https://www.bitdoze.com)), operated by **Dragos Balota** in Romania.

## 1. Hosting and Server Logs

Bitdoze is served as a static website through Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request metadata (such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp) to deliver pages securely, mitigate abuse, and keep the site reliable.

## 2. Privacy-Friendly Analytics

Bitdoze uses **Plausible Analytics** (`an3.bitdoze.com`) to measure aggregate site traffic and outbound link clicks (including custom affiliate click events on `/go/` redirect pages).

- No advertising cookies or cross-site tracking identifiers are used.
- Analytics data is collected in aggregate to understand which tutorials and guides are useful.

## 3. Contact Form Inquiries

Messages submitted through `/contact/` or `/es/contact/` are delivered via **FormSubmit** (`formsubmit.co`) to [dragos@bitdoze.com](mailto:dragos@bitdoze.com).

- We collect only the name, email address, optional subject, and message you submit.
- This information is used solely to reply to your inquiry and is never sold or added to marketing lists without your consent.

## 4. Browser Storage (`localStorage`)

Bitdoze stores lightweight preferences locally in your browser via `localStorage`:

- **Theme preference** (`theme`): Remembers your light or dark mode choice.
- **Course progress**: Tracks lessons you mark as watched under `/courses/`.

You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

## 5. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Embeds

Some articles contain affiliate or referral links (including `/go/` redirect links and Amazon Associates links). When you click one of those links, you leave `bitdoze.com` and the destination merchant's privacy policy applies. We disclose affiliate relationships above the first affiliate link on each relevant page.

Embedded YouTube videos use a click-to-load preview thumbnail (`youtube-nocookie.com` / `youtube.com`). External player scripts and requests are only loaded after you interact with the video player.

## 6. Your Rights and Contact

Under European Union data protection law (GDPR) and applicable Romanian regulations, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent us via email or the contact form.

To exercise these rights or ask any privacy question, email [dragos@bitdoze.com](mailto:dragos@bitdoze.com).
