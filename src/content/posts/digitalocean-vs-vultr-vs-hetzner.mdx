
This gives you Geekbench CPU scores (single and multi core), fio disk benchmarks (4K random read/write and sequential), and network throughput. The disk and CPU numbers in the results tables come from here. For a deeper dive into benchmarking methodology, see [how to benchmark your own VPS](/benchmark-cloud-servers/).

</Tab>
<Tab name="WordPress benchmark">

I installed a WordPress benchmark plugin that runs a series of PHP and MySQL operations and returns a composite score (higher is better). Run the same plugin across all servers you want to compare; the absolute score matters less than the relative difference. Tests ran on a fresh WordPress install with the Kadence starter template on CloudPanel ([install CloudPanel on your VPS](/install-cloudpanel-host-nodejs/) if you want the same stack).

</Tab>

## Extra costs: backups, bandwidth & IPv4

The list price is never the whole story. Here's what the providers don't put in the headline comparison tables:

**Backups.** Vultr charges +20% of the base fee for automatic backups. DigitalOcean charges 20% of Droplet cost for backups. Hetzner charges 20% of the server price for automated backups, or €0.0143/GB/mo for snapshots. A "cheap" server with backups enabled costs more than the sticker price.

**Bandwidth overage.** Hetzner gives you 20 TB on EU shared plans, 1–5 TB on US CPX plans, and 0.5–5 TB on Singapore CPX plans; overage is €1.00/TB in EU/US and €7.40/TB in Singapore. DigitalOcean includes 500 GiB to 6 TB by plan size, and Vultr includes 0.5 TB to 15 TB depending on the plan (plus 2 TB of free pooled account bandwidth). Both DigitalOcean and Vultr bill outbound overages at $0.01/GB (~$10/TB); inbound traffic is free.

<Notice type="warning" title="Hetzner US & Singapore bandwidth limits">
If you deploy on Hetzner's US locations, included bandwidth starts at 1 TB (scaling up to 5–8 TB on larger plans) versus 20 TB in EU, and Singapore overages run €7.40/TB. If you serve heavy traffic to global users, either host in EU behind a CDN or budget for egress.
</Notice>

**IPv4 surcharge.** Hetzner prices primary IPv4 at €0.50/mo (excl. VAT), included in the headline rates on hetzner.com and deducted if you run IPv6-only. For anything that needs inbound IPv4 (mail servers, game servers, many API consumers), keep it enabled.

**DDoS protection.** Vultr charges $10/mo per instance for 10 Gbps DDoS mitigation. Hetzner and DO include basic protection at no extra cost.

**Hourly billing.** All three providers support hourly billing (DigitalOcean now bills per second with a 60-second minimum). Spin up, test, tear down. No lock-in.

At the 4 vCPU / 8 GB tier, Hetzner's CX33 is €8.49–8.99/mo versus DO's $48/mo — roughly 5x cheaper on Cost-Optimized tiers, and even Regular Performance CPX32 at €35.49–35.99/mo beats DO's $48/mo. DO charges for its managed services and ecosystem, not raw VPS specs.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Which VPS is best for WordPress?" group="faq">
Vultr Intel HF won our WordPress benchmark at 9.3 (vs Hetzner at 8.9 and DO AMD at 7.9). But that's raw uncached performance. For WordPress-per-dollar, Hetzner wins by a wide margin. Whichever you pick, add caching and a CDN on top. If you don't want to manage the server at all, managed WordPress hosting on Cloudways handles it for you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Does Hetzner charge for IPv4?" group="faq">
Yes. Hetzner prices a primary IPv4 address at €0.50/mo (excl. VAT). Their website now shows headline plan rates with primary IPv4 included, and selecting IPv6-only deducts €0.50/mo. If your workload can run IPv6-only, you save that fee; for anything needing inbound IPv4, keep it enabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Is Vultr faster than Hetzner?" group="faq">
On our benchmarks, Vultr's Intel High Frequency instances beat Hetzner's CPX11 on disk (410 vs 144 MB/s 4K) and CPU (1074/2165 vs 959/1863). But Hetzner's bigger plans close the gap per dollar. A Hetzner 4 vCPU / 8 GB box costs less than a 2 vCPU Vultr HF and delivers more total compute. The Hetzner vs Vultr choice depends on whether you need per-core speed or total resources.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Can I reproduce these VPS benchmarks myself?" group="faq">
Yes. Run `curl -sL https://yabs.sh | bash` for the disk and CPU benchmarks, and install a WordPress benchmark plugin on a fresh WP install for the application-level scores. PageSpeed and GTMetrix test the full page load. For more benchmarking tools and methodology, see [our VPS benchmarking guide](/benchmark-cloud-servers/). Spin up identical servers on each provider, run the same tests, and compare.
</Accordion>
