## The one thing to sort out first

Zigbee sensors talk to a **Zigbee coordinator**, not to a Home Assistant server directly. If you don't have one yet, the [Zigbee coordinator guide](/best-zigbee-coordinator-home-assistant/) covers the $35.90 SONOFF MG24 stick (and PoE options) that run most people's meshes, while the [Home Assistant hardware guide](/best-home-assistant-hardware/) covers the server itself.

The other half of range complaints is mesh routing: battery sensors are *end devices*, so they sleep and don't relay packets for neighbors. Mains-powered devices (smart plugs, bulbs, and the [ThirdReality plug 4-pack](/go/thirdreality-zigbee-plug-4pack/) at $38.99) act as **routers** that repeat signals for everything else. Two or three plugs in central hallways fix most "sensor in the garage won't stay paired" complaints before they happen.

## FAQ

<Accordion label="Zigbee vs Wi-Fi sensors: why does it matter?" group="faq" expanded="true">
Zigbee sensors run on coin cells for 2–5 years because the protocol wakes in milliseconds, sends a tiny packet, and goes back to sleep. Wi-Fi battery sensors take seconds to associate with an AP, burn through batteries in months, clutter your router's client table, and usually want a vendor cloud account. Zigbee devices join a purely local mesh that works when your internet is down; the only trade-off is buying a ~$36 coordinator stick once.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="ZHA or Zigbee2MQTT: which should I use?" group="faq">
Both support every sensor on this page. ZHA is built directly into Home Assistant and is the fastest way to start. Zigbee2MQTT exposes more device-specific settings (like the FP1E's radar parameters and Aqara P1 sensitivity), adds support for brand-new hardware faster, and keeps your Zigbee network independent of HA restarts. The [coordinator guide](/best-zigbee-coordinator-home-assistant/) has the full setup walkthrough.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Why does my sensor keep dropping off the network?" group="faq">
Almost always one of three things: the coordinator dongle is plugged straight into a USB 3 port without an extension cable (2.4GHz noise drowns Zigbee), the battery sensor is too far from the nearest mains-powered Zigbee router (add smart plugs between them), or an Aqara sensor was paired near the coordinator and then carried across the house instead of being paired in its final room.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Do I need a motion sensor AND a door sensor on every entry?" group="faq">
Rarely both on the same spot. Contact sensors give you instant, zero-false-positive "the door just opened" triggers (ideal for exterior doors, garage doors, and closets). Motion sensors cover interior rooms and hallways where doors stay open.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Are Aqara sensors worth the pairing quirks?" group="faq">
Yes. They pair directly to Zigbee2MQTT and ZHA without an Aqara hub, cost ~$14 each in the 3-pack, and are smaller than almost any competing contact sensor. Just pair them in their final location through a reliable repeater (like the ThirdReality plugs) and they stay connected for years.
</Accordion>
