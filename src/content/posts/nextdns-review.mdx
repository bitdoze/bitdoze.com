## What does NextDNS actually do?

NextDNS replaces your ISP's plaintext DNS resolver with an encrypted DoH, DoT, or DoQ resolver that checks every domain lookup against your chosen security feeds and ad blocklists before answering. Blocked ad, tracker, and malware domains return an empty answer so the payload never loads.

Because filtering happens at the DNS layer, every device on your network receives the same protection. Smart TVs, game consoles, printers, and IoT sensors that cannot run a browser extension get the exact same blocklists as your laptop.

## Which NextDNS features matter most?

NextDNS groups its controls into four practical tabs: Security (11+ threat-protection toggles), Privacy (84 curated blocklists plus OS telemetry blocking), Parental Control (category filters and schedules), and Settings (log residency, Rewrites, and EDNS anonymization).

| Feature | What it blocks or prevents |
|---|---|
| Threat Intelligence Feeds | Domains flagged for malware, phishing, or command-and-control traffic |
| Google Safe Browsing | Malicious sites from Google's threat database |
| Cryptojacking Protection | Browser-based cryptocurrency mining scripts |
| DNS Rebinding Protection | External domains trying to resolve private LAN IP addresses |
| IDN Homograph Protection | Lookalike domains using spoofed Unicode characters |
| Typosquatting Protection | Common misspellings of popular domains registered by scammers |
| DGA Protection | Algorithmically generated domains used by botnets |

| CSAM Protection | Domains on CSAM blocklists |

## What breaks in day-to-day NextDNS operations?

Three operational issues matter once NextDNS runs your whole network: the free tier silently drops filtering after 300,000 queries, NextDNS has no public status page during outages, and aggressive lists occasionally block legitimate links.

- **Silent 300,000-query free-tier cliff:** A household with phones, a smart TV, and IoT gear burns through 300,000 queries in roughly two weeks. Past the cap, NextDNS stops blocking and resolves everything normally with zero warning banner. Poll the [NextDNS public API](https://nextdns.github.io/api/) weekly or upgrade to Pro.
- **No public status page:** There is no `status.nextdns.io`. If your network points exclusively at NextDNS and an outage hits, resolution stops until you switch. Run the NextDNS CLI (`v1.50.2`) locally for caching, or keep a fallback resolver configured.
- **YouTube ads and occasional false positives:** DNS cannot block in-stream YouTube video ads because YouTube serves them from video hostnames. Pair NextDNS with uBlock Origin Lite, Brave, or Firefox, and check the Logs tab to allowlist any legitimate service caught by strict lists.

<Notice type="warning" title="Plan a fallback resolver">

Do not leave a critical home or office network dependent on a single remote DNS endpoint without a fallback. Running the NextDNS CLI locally caches records in RAM and recovers automatically from stalled DoH sockets in `v1.49.0+`.

</Notice>

**Verdict:** Lowest-priced unlimited cloud DNS filter with strong blocklist choice, EU/Swiss log residency, and active CLI/iOS clients.
