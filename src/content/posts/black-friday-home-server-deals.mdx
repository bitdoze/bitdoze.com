## How to spot a real deal

Two tricks sellers run every November:

- **Inflated list prices.** The Anker Prime TB5 dock shows a $445.98 "list price" and a -22% badge, but its real street price has been ~$350–400 all year. The discount is smaller than it looks.
- **Merged listings.** Budget mini PCs (especially GMKtec) share one Amazon page across a dozen CPU variants. Confirm the selected configuration in the dropdown before checkout, because the price you see may belong to a different model.

Paste the product URL into a price-history tracker (camelcamelcamel, Keepa) and compare against the baseline in the tables below.

## FAQ

<Accordion label="Are Black Friday prices actually the lowest of the year?" group="faq" expanded="true">
For PC hardware and peripherals, usually yes, or within a few dollars of it. The exceptions are products launched in Q4 (first discounts come in spring) and items with inflated list prices where the "deal" is fake. The baselines above are the reference points.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Did I miss the best prices by skipping Prime Big Deal Days?" group="faq">
Probably not. October discounts on Amazon bestsellers (the CP1500 UPS, mainstream NAS boxes) typically repeat, not beat, in November. The bigger risk is stock: if an item hits its target during Black Friday week, buy it rather than waiting for Cyber Monday.
</Accordion>
