Shots.so is a free mockup generator that turns plain screenshots into polished device mockups. You don't need Figma or Photoshop. Drop an image in your browser, pick a device frame and a background, export.

It's been around since 2022 and it is popular with indie hackers and developers who want good-looking visuals without opening a design tool. The free tier covers most casual use: static PNG exports with no watermark. If you want 4K stills, WebP, or video, that's where the paid plans start.

## What Shots.so does

Shots.so takes a screenshot, image, or video and wraps it in a device frame (iPhone, MacBook, browser, iPad, Android phone, Apple Watch) on top of a customizable background. You can drop several items on one canvas, add shadows and VFX, then export the result as a static image or, on paid tiers, as video.

## Features

The panels below follow the order you meet them in the editor, roughly frame first, then style, effects, background, and layout.

### Device frames

The device library covers the usual suspects and then some: iPhone (including iPhone 17), Apple Watch (Series 8, Ultra, Watch 10 in 42/46 mm), iPad Pro 12.9, MacBook, iMac, Pixel 7 Pro, Nothing Phone (1), Surface Book, and browser frames for Chrome and Safari in light and dark plus Arc. Pick a device and the screenshot auto-fits the screen area. That is the "device mockup" part handled; an iPhone mockup and a browser mockup take the same number of clicks.

### Screenshot styles

How the screenshot looks inside the frame. Default is clean with no effects. Glass Light and Glass Dark add a frosted glass overlay, Liquid Glass is translucent, Inset Light and Inset Dark give the screen a recessed look, Outline draws a wireframe border, Border puts a solid border around the screen, and Shadow drops a shadow on the device.

Two more controls live here that older write-ups skip: corner radius (Sharp, Curved, Round) and shadow controls (None, Spread, Hug, Adaptive, plus opacity). Together they are enough to match most screenshot mockup styles without touching a design tool.

### Effects and scenes

Effects cover VFX overlays (noise, VHS, glitch) and 3D shapes you can drop into the composition. Shadow scenes are presets for no shadow, device shadow, or shapes. The custom watermark option puts your logo or brand on the export, and it is a paid feature.

### Backgrounds

This is where Shots.so stands out from the pack of mockup tools:

- **Solid and gradient** colors with full customization
- **Glass** backgrounds with blur
- Preset collections: By Paper, Cosmic, Mystic, Desktop, Abstract, Earth, Radiant, Texture
- Unsplash integration to search and use stock photos in place
- Magic backgrounds generated by AI from your uploaded media's colors and style (live variants for video)
- Transparent export with no background, useful for compositing

Magic backgrounds run server-side on the media you uploaded. If you want fully local control over AI-generated art instead, [generate AI images locally on your Mac](/ai-images-mac/) and drop the result in as a custom background.

<Notice type="info" title="What leaves your machine">
Your screenshot is uploaded to Shots' backend for processing and export. Fine for marketing shots and public UIs. Think twice before pasting client dashboards, internal admin panels, or anything with secrets on screen.
</Notice>

### Layout presets and aspect ratios

Magic Preset suggests layouts automatically. You can also lock an aspect ratio: 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 9:16. Social presets for Instagram, X/Twitter, and Pinterest handle the social media mockup sizes so you are not measuring pixels yourself.

### Animated mockups and video export

Motion presets, video zoom, and parallax (60 fps on Pro) turn a static mockup into a short clip. That is useful for Product Hunt galleries, changelog posts, and landing page hero loops. Honest cap summary: Plus gives you 720p video, 10 exports, up to 1 minute each; Pro goes to 2K at 60 fps, unlimited exports, up to 3 minutes each. GIF (30 s cap, transparency supported) and transparent WebM video (1080p at 45 fps, up to 20 s) are Pro-only. Full numbers sit in the export matrix below.

Video export is the main reason most people end up paying. If what you need is a real screen recording with automatic zoom editing rather than mockup animation, use [Screen Studio for macOS](https://go.bitdoze.com/screen-studio) instead.

## How to use Shots.so (quick start)

<Tabs>
<Tab name="Web app">
1. Go to [shots.so](https://shots.so/). No signup required to start.

The whole process takes under a minute once you know the interface.

</Tab>
<Tab name="Mobile apps (iOS/Android)">

**Verify your export.** Open the downloaded file and check three things: it is a PNG at the scale you picked, nothing (like a watermark) is burned in that you did not ask for, and transparent background exports have an alpha channel. On Linux or macOS:

```bash
file mockup.png
# PNG image data, 3840 x 2160, 8-bit/color RGBA
identify mockup.png   # ImageMagick: confirms the alpha channel exists
```

"Broken" looks like: the expected dimensions missing, an opaque black background where transparency should be, or a watermark on a free-plan export.

**Common snags:**

- If the export button is greyed out, you hit a plan cap. 4K is Plus-gated, and 6K, WebP, GIF, and full video are Pro-gated. Drop the resolution or the format to stay on free
- If a Magic background will not generate, retry once, then fall back to a preset pack. Magic runs server-side, so it can fail independently of the editor
- If a feature is missing in the Safari extension or mobile app, use the web app. The full panel set lives there
- If large video uploads drag the editor, flip the Performance Mode toggle

## FAQ

<Accordion label="Is Shots.so really free?" group="faq" expanded="true">
Yes, for static mockups. The free tier exports PNG at 1x/2x scale, with every device frame, background, and style available, and no watermark. Video is capped or possibly unavailable on free, so check the export panel while logged out. 4K exports need Plus; WebP, GIF, and full video need Pro.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Does Shots.so add a watermark?" group="faq">
Not on free exports. Watermarks are inverted: adding your own logo or brand watermark is a paid (Pro) feature. Free static exports come out clean.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="What export formats does Shots.so support?" group="faq">
PNG on all tiers. WebP export and GIF (30 s cap, transparency) are Pro-only. Video runs from a limited free/Plus tier (720p, 30 fps) up to 2K at 60 fps on Pro, plus transparent WebM at 1080p/45 fps (20 s max) on Pro. See the export formats and limits matrix above.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Can I use Shots.so for App Store screenshots?" group="faq">
Not practically. There is no batch export for the full set of App Store and Google Play sizes. Screenhance or AppLaunchFlow are the right tools for store sets and launch kits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Does Shots.so work on phones?" group="faq">
Yes. iOS and Android apps exist with the full feature set in a compact form (check the in-app menu on shots.so for store links), plus a Safari extension since October 2024. The web app still has the deepest panel set.
</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Is my screenshot uploaded anywhere?" group="faq">
Yes. Your media is uploaded to Shots' backend for processing and export, and Magic backgrounds are generated server-side. Fine for marketing shots and public UIs. Do not paste client dashboards, internal admin panels, or screens with secrets on them.
</Accordion>

## Conclusion

As a review verdict: Shots.so is the fastest free mockup generator I know of for one-off, good-looking screenshot mockups. The free tier covers casual use with no watermark. Plus at $10/mo is the value pick for regular 4K stills. Pro at $15/mo only pays off if you ship video or WebP on a schedule.

It will not replace a full design workflow, and it will not produce App Store submission sets. For "I need a nice mockup for this tweet or blog post tonight," it remains hard to beat on speed. Drafts & Folders and a Text tool are on the roadmap, which will close some of the remaining gaps.

One last tip: after export, if you need vector logos or icons to composite over your mockup, [convert images to SVG for free](/convert-images-to-svg/) first.
