## Bot Mode changes the bill

Bot Mode ships in current Hermes Desktop builds (v0.23.4 on November 27, 2026) and turns profiles into a roster of Bots. Four parts of it matter for cost.

**Each Bot pins its own model.** In New Agent, Advanced, the model and provider pin takes any pair Hermes knows, and unset means inherit from the launch profile. That gives you the cheap pattern: one strong orchestrator Bot on GLM 5.3 or MiMo V2.6 Pro, and worker Bots on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or GLM 5.3 Flash. The orchestrator decides, the workers spend cents.

**Group chats multiply turns.** A room holds 2 to 6 Bots. Your message triggers up to three serial rounds, capped at 10 messages per send. Each member's turn carries every room message since its own last turn, up to about 200 messages or 32,000 characters. On a gateway-owned room, hitting the cap pauses the room and offers Continue, up to five more budgets. The Hermes docs give no token figure for a round, so do the math for your own rooms: messages per send, times context per turn, times the output price of every member.

**Bot-to-bot loops have a guard.** A Bot messages another with `message_agent`, and the reply back is notify-only. Past `gateway.bot_loop_guard.max_hops` (default 4) the guard refuses a further call, and it refuses the same message to the same teammate twice in one exchange. That default is your spend ceiling per exchange.

```yaml
gateway:
  bot_loop_guard:
    max_hops: 4
```

**Route group messages with a cheap model.** On gateway-owned rooms, "Pick who answers automatically" makes one short tool-less model call per unmentioned message. Send it to a cheap model with `auxiliary.group_router`, under `hermes model`, auxiliary, Group router. At $0.05/M input, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is the obvious model for that job.

Two cost controls that come free with the design: a Bot speaks only when it has something new to add, and hiding a Bot from the roster does not stop its routines. Check `hermes cron list` for jobs named `[bot:<name>]` before you wonder where the tokens went. The [setup guide](/hermes-agent-setup-guide/) has the full Bot Mode walkthrough.

<Accordion label="Does Bot Mode cost more?" group="faq">
It can. Each Bot pins its own model, so put a strong model on the orchestrator and a cheap one on the workers. Group chats run up to 3 rounds and 10 messages per send, and bot-to-bot exchanges stop at `gateway.bot_loop_guard.max_hops` (default 4). Route the "who answers" call to a cheap model with `auxiliary.group_router`.
</Accordion>

</Accordion>

</Accordion>
