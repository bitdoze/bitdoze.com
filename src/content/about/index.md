I'm Dragos, a DevOps engineer and technical writer with over a decade in IT. I write [bitdoze.com](https://www.bitdoze.com/), a site of tested, copy-paste-ready guides on self-hosting, Docker, Linux, homelab hardware, and AI tooling — every command and config on it is one I've run myself.

I also run [wpdoze.com](https://www.wpdoze.com/), a WordPress site with practical tutorials and reviews, and a [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@webdoze) where the same guides show up as video walkthroughs and courses.

My day-to-day stack is Docker, Proxmox, Traefik, Dokploy and a rack of mini PCs in a home lab — most Bitdoze articles come straight out of that lab or from client work. Off the clock I'm usually travelling or watching a film.
